Contributing to AI INVESTOR OS

Thank you for your interest in AI INVESTOR OS.

We aim to build an open and collaborative ecosystem around artificial intelligence, financial research and quantitative analysis.

Current Project Status

AI INVESTOR OS is currently in its early concept and architecture stage.

We welcome discussions, ideas, technical feedback and expressions of interest from developers and researchers.

Areas of Interest

We are particularly interested in contributions related to:

* Multi-agent AI systems
* LLM orchestration
* Financial data engineering
* Quantitative research
* Risk management
* Python development
* Financial technology
* Developer tooling

How to Get Involved

You can contribute by:

1. Exploring the project vision and roadmap.
2. Opening an issue to suggest an idea.
3. Discussing potential technical approaches.
4. Identifying problems or opportunities.
5. Sharing relevant research and resources.

Contribution Principles

We value:

* Clear documentation.
* Reproducible research.
* Respectful technical discussions.
* Transparent development.
* Responsible use of AI.
* Reliable financial data.

Before Contributing Code

Please discuss substantial changes before submitting code.

Contribution guidelines and development standards will evolve as the project moves from concept to implementation.

Community

We welcome developers, researchers and open-source enthusiasts interested in collaborative AI and financial intelligence.

Let’s build open financial intelligence together.
